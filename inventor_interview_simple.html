Inventor Interview: The Airplane Seat Belt

Invention: Airplane Seat Belt  |  Inventor: Benjamin Foulois

선정 이유

발명품: I chose the airplane seat belt because it is simple but saves many lives.

발명가: I chose Benjamin Foulois because he solved a real problem from his own experience.

Interview

Interviewer: Hello, Mr. Foulois. Thank you for joining us today.

Foulois: Thank you for having me. I'm happy to be here.

Q1. Why did you invent the airplane seat belt?

A1. I invented the airplane seat belt because I didn't want pilots to fall out of planes.

Q2. What does your invention do?

A2. My seat belt keeps passengers safe in their seats and reduces injuries from sudden turbulence.

Q3. What difficulty did you face, and how did you overcome it?

A3. I almost fell out, so I tied myself to the seat with a leather strap.

Q4. How will your invention change the future?

A4. Seat belts make flying safer, and future sensors may check if people wear them correctly.

Q5. Why are seat belts so important?

A5. Seat belts are necessary because passengers can get badly hurt when turbulence happens.

Interviewer: Thank you for your time.

Foulois: My pleasure.
